Method: 本文利用$\widetilde\Lambda$-不变量构造Laurent族的交换矩阵，引入$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量刻画交换对（当且仅当$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量为1），并借助Cao-Fei的热带$F$-不变量比较$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量与一般Ext不变量的关系，从而证明Laurent族是完全Λ-可容许的monoidal种子，并推进实单模对应丛单项式的猜想。

Result: 作者证明最大可交换实素模族总是Laurent族，Laurent族总是完全Λ-可容许的monoidal种子；交换对的充要条件是$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量为1。对于acyclic型丛代数的monoidal范畴化，证明了$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量不小于一般Ext不变量，且实单模恰好对应丛单项式的猜想成立。

Conclusion: 本文在量子仿射代数与箭图Hecke代数的monoidal范畴中建立了丛倾斜理论的大量类似结论，统一了Laurent族与完全Λ-可容许monoidal种子的概念，并通过$\mathfrak{d}$-不变量与热带F-不变量刻画了交换对及实单模与丛单项式的对应关系，深化了对monoidal范畴化的结构理解。

Abstract: In this paper, we prove that many analogous facts of cluster-tilting theory holds in monoidal categories from quantum affine algebras or quiver Hecke algebras. We show that a maximal commuting family of real prime modules, which is an analogous object of a cluster-tilting object, is always a laurent family introduced by Kashiwara-Kim-Oh-Park. By using $\widetildeΛ$-invariants, we construct an exchange matrix of each laurent family and show that a laurent family is always completely $Λ$-admissible monoidal seed. We obtain that a pair of real prime modules is an exchange pair if and only if $\mathfrak{d}$-invariant is $1$.
  When the category is a monoidal categorification of a cluster algebra of acyclic type, we prove that $\mathfrak{d}$-invariant is not less than generic Ext invariant by using tropical $F$-invariant introduced by Cao and Fei. Moreover, we show that, the conjecture asserting that real simple modules correspond exactly to cluster monomials holds in these cases.
