TL;DR: 该论文将plabic tangles定义的几何提升映射的簇理论视角扩展到两个新方向：一是引入量子提升(quantum promotions)，猜想所有由dominant solvable tangles定义的提升都可自然提升为量子簇拟同态；二是定义簇-Lusztig拟同态，证明经典和量子m-一元星提升属于该类别，并利用非solvable核心研究amplituhedron胞面的面，反驳了T. Lam的一个猜想。

Motivation: 此前的工作(《Tangles》)提出了一个主要猜想：当tangle是dominant且solvable时，诱导的代数提升是簇拟同态。本文旨在将这一簇理论视角从经典扩展到量子层面，同时引入与Lusztig对偶典范基相关的更强结构概念，以更深入地理解提升映射的性质，并利用这些工具研究amplituhedron胞面的几何特性。

Method: 本文方法包括：(1)定义量子提升并猜想所有dominant solvable tangles的提升可自然提升为量子簇拟同态，并验证文献中所有已证明为经典簇拟同态的案例；(2)定义簇-Lusztig拟同态（将Lusztig对偶典范基元素映射到对偶典范基元素，允许冻结变量中的Laurent单项式），并证明经典和量子m-一元星提升属于此类；(3)利用intersection number大于1的非solvable核心研究amplituhedron胞面的面结构。

Result: 该文证明了经典和量子m-一元星提升满足簇-Lusztig拟同态性质，这是一个意外结果；通过非solvable核心研究了amplituhedron胞面的面，展示了某些面不在对偶典范基元素的零轨迹中，从而反驳了T. Lam提出的猜想。同时验证了所有已知的经典簇拟同态案例在量子情形下也成立。

Conclusion: 本文在量子层面扩展了plabic tangles提升的簇理论视角，提出了量子簇拟同态猜想和簇-Lusztig拟同态猜想，并通过对amplituhedron胞面性质的研究反驳了T. Lam的猜想，为后续深入研究plabic tangles与簇代数、量子群以及amplituhedron之间的联系提供了新方向。

Abstract: Plabic tangles define geometric promotion maps between configurations and, after choosing a lift, between Grassmannians. The main conjecture in \cite{Tangles} is that when the tangle is dominant and solvable the induced algebraic promotion is cluster quasi-homomorphism. This work extends the cluster perspective in two directions. First, we initiate the study of quantum promotions. We conjecture that all promotions defined from dominant solvable tangles are naturally lifted to a \emph{quantum cluster quasi homomorphism}, and prove for all cases in the literature that the were shown to be (classical) cluster quasi-homomorphisms. Secondly, we define the notion of cluster-Lusztig quasi-homomorphisms, which are cluster quasi homomorphisms that take Lustig's dual canonical basis elements to dual canonical basis element, up to a Laurent monomial in frozen variables, with clearing and normalization chosen uniformly on each content component. We prove the unexpected result that the classical and quantum $m$-unary star promotions fall into this category, and conjecture that all promotions coming from dominant solvable tangles share the same property. We then consider promotions via non solvable cores of intersection number higher than one. We use it to study facets of amplituhedron cells, and to show that some of them do not lie in zero loci of dual canonical basis elements, disproving a conjecture by T. Lam \cite{LamGP}.
