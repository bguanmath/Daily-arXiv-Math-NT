### [9] [Quantum Weyl Relations arising from Two-Term Complexes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22124)
*Qinghua Chen,Yonggang Hu*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文研究有限域上Dynkin箭图的两-term复形extriangulated范畴的平方根正规化Hall代数，建立了PBW型向量空间分解，证明了关键的交换关系z_i p_i = q^{-1}p_i z_i + 1，确定了所有非对角积，并用p_i和z_i生成了量子Weyl代数和Hall-Fock模。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为Donaldson-Thomson理论中的范畴Hall型Weyl作用提供有限域的Hall模型，并研究两-term复形范畴的代数结构。

Method: 研究extriangulated范畴，建立Ringel-Hall部分和shifted-projective部分的PBW型分解，导出混合乘法公式，利用BGP反射函子进行传输。

Result: 证明了z_i p_i = q^{-1}p_i z_i + 1，确定了非对角积z_i p_j由核与余核刻画，每对(p_i,z_i)生成秩一量子Weyl代数，正交投射族生成高秩量子Weyl子代数，构造了Hall-Fock模，p_i作用为产生算子，z_i作用为q-湮灭算子，BGP反射保持反射子代数和混合Hall系数。

We first establish a PBW-type vector-space factorization into the Ringel--Hall part and the shifted-projective part, and derive an explicit mixed multiplication formula. For the indecomposable projectives $P_i$, let $p_i$ and $z_i$ be the normalized Hall classes of $(0\to P_i)$ and $(P_i\to0)$, respectively. We prove \[
  z_ip_i=q^{-1}p_iz_i+1, \] and determine all off-diagonal products $z_ip_j$ in terms of kernels and cokernels of maps $P_i\to P_j$. For these subalgebras we construct explicit Hall--Fock modules, on which the $p_i$ act as creation operators and the $z_i$ act as $q$-annihilation operators. This provides a finite-field Hall model parallel to categorical Hall-type Weyl actions in Donaldson--Thomas theory. Finally, we show that BGP reflection transports the corresponding reflection subalgebras and preserves the mixed Hall coefficients.
